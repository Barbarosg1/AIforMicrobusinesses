I’m pleased to share our new whitepaper, “AI Integration in Turkish Microbusinesses: Challenges, Solutions, and a Pilot-Ready Playbook.”

Developed through a collaboration between Microsoft Türkiye and Koç University CEMSMIM students, the study draws on 41 in-person interviews 
with microbusiness owners across Istanbul. It explores a practical question: how can AI move beyond abstract promise and 
help small businesses address everyday challenges such as stock management, staffing, pricing, accounting, and customer follow-up?

The findings point to a clear opportunity. Adoption will not be driven by technology alone, but by focused solutions that deliver 
measurable value, integrate with tools businesses already use, and earn trust through transparency and owner control. 
The whitepaper translates these insights into a pilot-ready playbook for a mobile-first business agent designed around the 
realities of microbusiness operations in Türkiye.
